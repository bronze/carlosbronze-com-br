## The itch

I watch a lot of [Critical Role](https://critrole.com/), and their homepage works like a catalog: everything carries the same weight, and visitors have to figure out on their own where to start. Weekly viewers, newcomers and potential subscribers all get the same list.

## What I did

Instead of treating the page as a catalog, I structured the navigation as a decision system, making clear what people need first, what they explore next, and where the subscription fits in naturally.

<Steps items={[
  { title: "What's on now", text: "Watch the latest episode and this week's schedule, with what's live today." },
  { title: 'Catch up and explore', text: 'Recaps for anyone who missed an episode, and every campaign, from the first to the current one, for newcomers.' },
  { title: 'Subscribe', text: 'Beacon shows up after people have seen what the content is worth, with clear benefits and a free trial.' },
]} />

## The result

A simpler, more intentional structure that lowers cognitive load and does a better job of guiding engagement: each block on the page answers a visitor's question, in the order it comes up.
