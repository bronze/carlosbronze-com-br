## The itch

Every [Papo na Arena](https://open.spotify.com/show/7lcBkPYn5HgEZjTkJhNUFJ) episode ends with the products of the week: tools, apps and services the guests are using. It's one of my favourite parts, but in audio they get lost. Weeks later, all that's left is the question: what was that tool someone mentioned?

## The insight

Every recommendation has the same three pieces: an episode, a person and a product. Write that down in a structured way and the rest comes for free. The ranking, the categories and each person's history are just different ways of looking at the same list.

## What I did

I built the site on Replit and started logging the mentions by hand after each episode airs. From that list, the site offers four ways to browse:

Since it's a fan project, the site says plainly that it isn't affiliated with the podcast or with Product Arena.

## What I learned

> Before thinking about the interface, think about the shape of the data.

With the list well structured, each new view of the site became a filter, not another page to maintain.
